对我们的意义What this means for our work

值得做的， 不是追每个热点。Choose experiments, not every headline.

以下为工程建议，不代表已部署或实测。Engineering recommendations, not deployment or benchmark claims.

01
Agent：把权限与验收做进流程Agents: enforce permissions and acceptance

优先评估 Skills 的依赖与版本、工具调用权限、失败恢复、可追溯证据。Evaluate Skill dependencies, versioning, tool permissions, recovery and traceable evidence.

02
NPU：迁移方法，逐项验证实现NPU: transfer methods, verify implementations

借鉴混合精度、缓存复用和分离式训练推理；不能把 CUDA / ROCm 发布当作昇腾已适配。Study mixed precision, caching and disaggregated training/inference. CUDA or ROCm releases do not establish Ascend support.

03
研究：先选一个可证伪的小目标Research: start with one falsifiable target

固定模型、硬件、负载与质量门槛，再测真实收益。量子、制药与聚变保持研究跟踪。Fix model, hardware, load and quality constraints before measuring gains. Track quantum, drug discovery and fusion as research.
